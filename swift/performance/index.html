Performance

Diagnose and fix real performance problems — memory, CPU, rendering, startup, networking and energy — with symptoms, diagnostics and fixes for each.

Intermediate → Advanced 16 articles
#performance #instruments #optimization

Performance work is empirical. You measure, form a hypothesis, change one thing, and measure again. This section pairs each topic with the symptoms you can observe, the diagnostics that confirm the cause, and the fixes that actually move the number.

Start with Instruments Overview and Time Profiler, then jump to the symptom you have: scrolling jank, memory growth, slow launch, or energy drain. Every article assumes you are measuring on a real device — the Simulator distorts CPU, GPU and memory behaviour.
