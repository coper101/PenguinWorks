Architecture is the set of decisions that are expensive to reverse. This section avoids dogma — no pattern is "correct" in isolation. Instead, each article explains what a pattern optimises for, what it costs, and when a simpler approach is better.

Start with **MVC, MVVM, MVP, VIPER and Clean Architecture** for the landscape, then **Dependency Injection** and **Testable Application Design**, which are the two practices that most improve real codebases regardless of the pattern on the label. **Modular Architecture** and **Architecture Trade-offs** are where senior interviews are won.
