How Echo Works

> 1. Transmit a signal.

> 2. Receive an Echo.

> 3. Keep it in your archive.

> 4. Optionally cast the transmission + Echo into The Void.

> 5. Pulse public Echoes.

> 6. Your transmission allowance depends on the active terminal.

Transmission Allowances

> Echo uses one universal 24-hour orbit for every terminal. Your transmission allowance is how many transmissions you can send within an orbit.

> Orbital-24: 1 transmission per 24-hour orbit.

> Nebula-12: 5 transmissions per 24-hour orbit.

> Rewards may increase the transmissions available to you when applicable.

The Void

> When you cast an Echo into The Void, your transmission and its Echo become public and are shown with an anonymous public Beacon identifier. Public Void content may also appear on the Echo website.

> Do not cast content that contains personal or confidential information.

Can't Transmit?

> Check your connection and confirm you have transmissions remaining in the current orbit.

> If you have been blocked, you may contact support to request review.
